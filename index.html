Biography

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, Nigeria, and currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at North-West University, South Africa. My academic journey began at ABU Zaria, where I completed my advanced training, culminating in an MSc and PhD in Applied Mathematics.

My work sits at the intersection of mathematical physics, thermal sciences, and high-performance computing. I focus on designing and implementing numerical frameworks to solve highly non-linear, coupled boundary layer formulations modeling complex multiphysics flow systems.

Research Specialties

Multiphysics Fluid Dynamics

Modeling magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), electroosmotic flow fields, and nanofluid dynamics in specialized micro-channels.

Advanced Spectral Solvers

Expert development and implementation of SCM, BSQLM, and SQLM algorithms to resolve highly coupled boundary problems.

Thermal Transport in 3D-ICs

Designing enhanced mathematical cooling topologies for microfluidic energy harvesters and semiconductor stacks.

Deep Learning & PINNs

Integrating standard numerical solvers with Physics-Informed Neural Networks in Python to accelerate multi-parameter thermal models.
