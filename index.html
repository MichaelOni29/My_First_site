Biography

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, Nigeria, and currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at North-West University, South Africa. My academic journey began at ABU Zaria, where I completed my A-level, MSc, and PhD training in applied mathematics.

My work sits at the intersection of mathematical physics and high-performance computing. I focus on developing advanced numerical frameworks to solve highly non-linear, coupled boundary layer formulations modeling thermal management challenges in emerging microsystems.

Research Specialties

Multiphysics Fluid Dynamics

Modeling magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), electroosmotic frameworks, and nanofluid dynamics in highly micro-confined channels.

Advanced Spectral Solvers

Expert development and implementation of SCM, BSQLM, and SQLM schemes to resolve stiff, non-linear boundaries.

Thermal Transport in 3D-ICs

Designing enhanced mathematical cooling topologies for integrated electronics and enhanced microfluidic energy harvesters.

Deep Learning & PINNs

Integrating standard numerical solvers with Physics-Informed Neural Networks in Python to pioneer hybrid thermal simulations.
